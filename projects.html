Research Project

Digital Documentation of Byzantine Churches in Laconia

This project aims to digitally document and analyze more than one hundred Byzantine churches located in the Laconia region of southern Greece. The research combines photogrammetry-based 3D modeling with historical, architectural, and art historical analysis.

By creating high-resolution 3D models of these churches, the project seeks to integrate inscriptions, wall paintings, and architectural features into a single digital environment. This approach enables a comprehensive study of their spatial configuration, visual programs, and historical context.

The digital archive will serve both as a scholarly resource and as a platform for public dissemination, contributing to the preservation and reinterpretation of Byzantine cultural heritage in the Peloponnese.
