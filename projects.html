Projects

Byzantine Churches of Laconia

PERIOD
2019–
REGION
Laconia, Peloponnese, Greece
METHODS
Photogrammetry; 3D scholarly editions
PI
Koji Murata (University of Tsukuba)
TEAM
Koji Murata, Ryo Higuchi, and others

The project documents more than one hundred Byzantine churches in Laconia with photogrammetric 3D models. By bringing architecture, wall paintings and inscriptions together in a single digital environment, it treats each church as a research resource for historians, art historians and architectural historians, and develops 3D scholarly editions that combine visual and textual evidence. It is carried out as a team project and has been supported by the Kajima Foundation since 2022.
